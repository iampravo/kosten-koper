contract clauses to insist on
financing contingency voorbehoud financiering — walk away free if your mortgage falls through, typically a 6-week window
survey contingency voorbehoud bouwkundige keuring — near-essential on anything built before ~1990
NHG contingency if financing with NHG, make the offer conditional on NHG approval too
structural risks
wooden pile foundation ~750,000 NL homes built before 1970 have one — higher risk in peat/clay soil (Amsterdam, Rotterdam, Utrecht, Gouda, Zaanstreek)
asbestos common in anything built or renovated before 1994
roof, insulation, pipework lead water pipes turn up in pre-1960s homes; check roof age and isolation on the survey
legal & ownership status
erfpacht (leasehold) check the canon amount, whether it's fixed/perpetual (eeuwigdurend afgekocht) or resets, and the end date — this alone can swing monthly cost a lot
VvE health (apartments) reserve fund size vs. the maintenance plan (MJOP), and recent meeting minutes for disputes or upcoming special assessments
monument status restricts renovations and can require permits even for small changes
boundaries & easements erfdienstbaarheid / erfgrens — confirm what's actually yours and who has rights of way
financial & admin
WOZ value vs. asking price a big gap either way is worth asking about — it also drives your eigenwoningforfait
permits for past work vergunningen for any extension or major renovation — unpermitted work complicates insurance, resale, and financing
soil contamination bodemverontreiniging — worth checking near former industrial sites or if there was ever an underground oil tank
